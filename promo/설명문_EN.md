How this ad copy was written

This copy was not hand-picked. Eight human-written candidates were shown to 25 synthetic
population personas who were asked whether they would click and what held them back, then
evolved over five generations. The English score rose from 37.12 to 43.84.

Read that number carefully. Interest rose, but average trust in the claim fell from 3.44 to
2.48 over the same span, because the scoring function weights stated click intent far more
heavily than trust. These are simulated scores predicted by an AI, not measured click-through
rates. The sample was limited to adults 22 and over in professional, research or technical
work or with higher education. The code and the sample are public:
